Read the opposition report state by state

The Sabin Center at Columbia Law School keeps a record of every state and local restriction on wind, solar and storage siting, and every project that has met organized local opposition. This page opens that record in your browser, filters it down to the states and technologies you care about, pulls out the news articles and documents each entry cites, and lets you mark what people objected to.

or drag it here — PDF, CSV or XLSX

  1. Go to oppositionreport.org and download the data.
  2. The restriction and contested project files read most cleanly — they are already in columns. Open both at once if you have them.
  3. The full report PDF also works. It has to be cut apart by pattern, so a few entries come through ragged; every record keeps its original text so you can check it.

Files are read on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and your tagging is saved in this browser.
